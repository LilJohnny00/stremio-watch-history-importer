Stremio Watch History Importer

Bring your old watch history from Netflix, Crunchyroll, Disney+ & Prime Video into Trakt — which then syncs to Stremio. Everything runs locally on your PC.

Before you start

  1. Create a free Trakt account.
  2. Sign up for Trakt VIP. Only needed so you can create the API application used in Step 1. Once you have finished importing everything, you can cancel the VIP subscription.

1. Connect Trakt

Create an app at trakt.tv/oauth/applications/new. In Redirect URIs put urn:ietf:wg:oauth:2.0:oob, leave the rest blank, click Create. Then paste the two values below.

5. Send it to Stremio

Your history is now on Trakt. To make it show in Stremio's library:
  1. Open stremio.com/acc-settings.
  2. Go to the Integrations tab and make sure Trakt is authenticated.
  3. Click the Trakt Import button (imports your whole Trakt history into Stremio; usable once every 24 hours).
