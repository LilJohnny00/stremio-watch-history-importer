Prime Video → Trakt: getting your history file

Prime Video has no download button, so this reads your Watch History page (which is per-profile) and pages through all of it. Just copy-paste; nothing is saved to your PC as a file.

Step 1. Open your Prime watch history

  1. Go to primevideo.com/settings/watch-history and make sure you are on your profile (top-right avatar).
  2. Press F12 to open Developer Tools, then click the Console tab.

Step 2. Copy the script (in the app)

  1. Back in the app, select the Amazon Prime tab and click Copy Prime script. (No values to enter for Prime.)

Step 3. Run it

  1. In the Console, paste the script and press Enter.
  2. If Chrome warns about pasting, type allow pasting, press Enter, then paste again. (That's a Chrome safety prompt, not a virus.)
  3. It prints page 1, page 2 … then downloads primevideo-history.json to your Downloads.

Step 4. Upload it

Back in the app (Amazon Prime selected), drag in primevideo-history.json. Then Preview → Import. Done! 🎉

Heads-up: some Prime rows only say "Season 1/2/3" with no show name (Prime's quirk). Those cannot be matched automatically — you can mark a handful watched by hand on Trakt if you care. Everything with a proper title matches well, and movie "quality" tags like (4K UHD) are handled automatically.
