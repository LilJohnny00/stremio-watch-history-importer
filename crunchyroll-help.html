Crunchyroll → Trakt: getting your history file

Crunchyroll has no download button, so we read it from your logged-in session. Just copy-paste, ~2 minutes. Nothing is sent anywhere except back to you as a file.

Step 1. Open your Crunchyroll history

  1. Go to crunchyroll.com/history and make sure you are logged in.
  2. Press F12 to open Developer Tools.
  3. Click the Network tab.

Step 2. Find your two values

  1. In the Network filter box, type: watch-history
  2. Press F5 to refresh. Click the watch-history?... row.
  3. In Headers, note:
    • Account ID — in the Request URL, the long id between /content/v2/ and /watch-history.
    • Authorization — the header starting with Bearer eyJ... (copy the whole value).

These expire in a few minutes — continue promptly. A 401 just means they expired; refresh and grab them again.

Step 3. Build & copy your script (in the app)

  1. Back in the app, select Crunchyroll, paste your Account ID and Authorization into the two boxes.
  2. Click Build my script, then Copy script. Your values are already inside.

Step 4. Run it

  1. Back on Crunchyroll, click the Console tab.
  2. Paste the copied script and press Enter.
  3. It downloads crunchyroll-history-full.json to your Downloads.

Step 5. Upload it

Back in the app (Crunchyroll selected), drag in crunchyroll-history-full.json. Then Preview → Import. Done! 🎉
